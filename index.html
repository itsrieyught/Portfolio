About Me

I'm a creative designer focused on making brands impossible to scroll past. I design static ads, email campaigns, and social media graphics in Canva and Figma, and I edit video ads and UGC content in CapCut with clean cuts, deliberate pacing, and smooth transitions.

My design work started on the business side. While running order fulfillment and customer support for a freelance reselling business, I also created the visuals and promo content for its products. That taught me to design with a goal in mind, not just for looks, and to stay organized and accurate under a heavy workload. Today I'm sharpening my craft through concept projects, designing ads, emails, and videos for products the way a real brand brief would demand. I learn new tools quickly and take full ownership of every project, from brief to final file.
